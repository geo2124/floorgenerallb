# Floorgenerallb

I want to create a game i will call: The Floor General. I want you to act as a top level UI/UX engineer who has a top tier experience in gamification. I want to create a prototype of the game with the main features mocks and the real layout & UI/UX.
The Floor General is a game where players will have to prepare their player for games, i want the design & experience of the player to be very advanced, very realistic and very high level graphically, i dont want a simple silhouette or cartoonish experience. This player will have multiple attributes to develop such as Shooting , Ball Handling, Speed .... these attributes are relative to the position he plays. These attributes are developed by spending xp points and each needs a time to finish, some few minutes some few hours depends on the level of upgrade. Xp points are earned by playing prediction games of live lebanese basketball games, it can be done by single game or win more with combos, also other types of predictions related, such as scorer of the game, top scorer of the round ..... Also another game is trivia which is answering questions about basketball in general, nba, euroleague or lebanese basketball, each topic offers levels and each level wins you Xp, but to play these trivias you need credits and to do prediction submissions you need credits as well, each credit gives you a trivia round or a prediction round. A player has 5 free credits every 10 hours (non cumulative, it just recharges every 10 hours, so 1 every 2 hours) player can buy credits or boost upgrade time by money or watching ads. Also every player has an album of real players photos, every day a play unpacks 5 cards of players, more packs can be bought with money only. These cards have levels (Example: Wael Arakji common, Wael Arakji Rare, Wael Arakji Epic).... players will have to fill the entire album of all the teams with coaches & players and they can download it as a PDF, once completed they can enter a draw for a big prize. These cards he can use them before every game to boost his player's attribute, each card gives + and - attribute (example : +5 3pts shooting +2 midrange shooting -1 Rebound), the player can change before the game start by 5 mins the cards he want to use in every game.
Now the game play, you can play 1 v 1 against players , you can set the strategy for your player example : shoot 60% 3s, 40% midrange, Defend the 3s more .... this can be played against random players and the winner generates XP points and the loser loses XP points. Second game play is taking part of a team, the manager is user as well with his own player in, the team will be consisting of 8 players, 5 starters on their positions and 3 bench players, also the coach will set the team strategy and every end of a quarter he can change within 30 seconds. The game play is very limited visually and fast, the whole game should take 1 minute & 30 seconds after every quarter to change, also a coach has 2 timeouts for the whole game where he has 30 seconds to change strategy or substitute players. a player can make it all automated so we can save time in game play so it generates instantly if both are on auto. 
Create the concept, understand that my main target is the design that needs to be high level, very simple interface and user friendly, easy to go through it and simple to understand, my main target is a top tier UI/UX , extremely attractive yet simple and smart.
i want you to make it look totally as a mobile application game, with sticky navigation and everything ...
